## Features

### Stream music from your Synology NAS

Aux is an audio player app for iOS that works with Audio Station running on your Synology NAS to stream your music collection from your private cloud.

### Offline audio player

Aux can download audio to play your music on the go without an internet connection.

### Lossless and high-resolution audio

Aux plays any audio file compatible with iOS, including lossless and high-resolution audio formats from `16-bit / 44.1 kHz` to `24-bit / 384 kHz` and beyond — only limited by your DAC.

- MP4 / AAC
- MP3
- Apple Lossless (ALAC)
- FLAC
- WAV
- Dolby Digital
- Dolby Digital Plus
- Dolby Atmos spatial audio
- And more, a complete list is available in the app settings

### Security & Privacy

Aux is a fast, ad-free, and feature-rich app.

Aux connects directly to your NAS. Since Aux uses no servers of its own, your username, password, and all other data are exchanged securely and directly with your NAS. 

If you've chosen not to expose your NAS directly to the internet, Aux also works over your home network or with Synology's own QuickConnect service to relay the connection through Synology's secure servers.

### Server Management

- Secure, encrypted HTTPS network connections
- Connect by QuickConnect, domain, DDNS, or IP address
- Secure, encrypted login with 2FA / OTP support
- Secure Keychain storage with Touch ID / Face ID protection
- Multiple server / NAS support
- Download mode for offline playback
- Streaming mode for real-time playback
- Dynamic, automatic reconnection when switching networks with QuickConnect
  - Roam from LAN to WAN and vice versa
- Download manager to pause, cancel, reorder, and restart downloads
- Smart management of background and audio download sessions to keep downloads going in the background

### Folder Browsing

- Audio Station folder browsing
- File Station folder browsing — view images and files, download audio formats not recognized by Audio Station
- Pinned folder support for quick access to favorites
- Search your NAS or downloaded files across artist, album, track title, or file / folder name
- Link sharing (DSM) and file export (iOS share sheet)
- Queue downloads of entire folders and subfolders
- View MP3 / MP4 / FLAC tags and audio metadata indexed by Audio Station
- View album and file artwork, tap to view fullscreen
- Build a playlist with replace, shuffle, insert, append
- Choose to download or stream audio by default and when adding items to your playlist

### Audio Player

- Play any audio format supported by iOS, including Dolby Audio formats not supported by Audio Station
- Gapless playback
- Lossless and high-resolution audio support
  - No forced transcoding to 16/48 WAV format for high resolution downloads, which you are normally forced to do with the first-party app
- Seek & skip with Siri, lock screen, headphone remote, or in-app controls
- Tap & hold to seek ahead / back instantly
  - No more waiting to seek while it replays audio you just heard
- Seek across track boundaries to hear the end of that last song one more time
- A/B song comparison
  - Tap to mark a time, then as you switch between tracks, all tracks start at the marked time
  - As this is power feature, enable it in the app settings
- Artwork view for album / file artwork
  - Tap to view images fullscreen
- Playlist view to manage queued tracks
- Metadata view for MP3 / MP4 / FLAC tags and audio metadata
- AirPlay output with bitstreaming / buffering options
- Shuffle / unshuffle, repeat track, repeat playlist
- Saved playlist and playback position across app launches

### User Interface

- Optimized for one-handed use on the go
  - Important controls are on the lower third of the screen
- Quick list controls
  - Tap & hold to enter edit mode
  - Two-finger drag to select
  - Buttons to select all / none, pin, share, add to playlist, download
- Haptic feedback for gestures and interactions
- Many customization options:
  - Keep-ahead `x` tracks for downloads / streams
  - Choose between skip or seek controls
  - Skip/seek by `x` seconds
  - Pause or duck audio on interruptions
  - AirPlay
    - URL-flinging / bitstreaming (for surround sound or spatial audio)
    - `24-bit / 48 kHz` ALAC buffered audio
  - Replace or append to the current playlist by default
- Written with the latest native Apple technologies from the ground up
  - Swiftr 6, SwiftUI, SwiftData, Swift String Concurrency, etc
