# Privacy Policy

**September 4th, 2024**

The privacy policy for each product or platform can be found below.

For purposes of this policy, "personally identifiable information" means any information that could be used to uniquely identify an individual person, including but not limited to name, e-mail address, mailing address, or phone number.


## Aux – Music app for your NAS

### What to know

- Aux uses RevenueCat to handle app subscriptions.
- Purchase history data collected from Aux is anonymous and not linked to your identity.

### What information is collected

- RevenueCat collects anonymized analytics and purchase history data to perform experiments, enable features, and track purchases.
- RevenueCat collects and validates transaction receipts to prevent fraud.

### What information is not collected

- No personally identifiable information is collected by the app or RevenueCat software.
- Your NAS server address, username, password, and other account information is not collected. All data is transmitted directly between your NAS and your mobile device, unless you choose to use the Synology QuickConnect service to relay the connection to your NAS, in which case your data flows through Synology's secure servers.

### More information

- [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- [Synology U.S. privacy policy](https://www.synology.com/en-us/company/legal/privacy)


## Website

### What to know

- This website uses web analytics software from Google Analytics to collect information on the use of the website and its features. This information is important to understand where to focus development efforts, or what should be improved or fixed.
- This website uses conversion tracking cookies to track clicks on Google AdWords ads that flow through to the site. This allows us to see the total number of users who have followed an ad onto key pages of the site, and therefore measure the efficacy of our advertising
- None of the information can be tied back to a specific user. All data is aggregated amongst all users of the website.

### What information is collected

- When pages are viewed, traffic information about the source and destination of the user is collected.
- When a user comes to the site from a Google AdWords ad and views certain pages on the site, the user is counted and aggregated with all other users that have done the same. 
- If you submit information to the website, including personally identifiable information, as in the case of a support request, the information you submit is collected.

### What information is not collected

- No personally identifiable information is collected by the website, conversion tracking, or analytics software.

### Opt out

- You may opt out of Google Analytics using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](http://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en).
- You may opt out of Google interest-based advertising using Google's [Advertising and Privacy](https://www.google.com/intl/en/privacy/ads) page.
- You may disable conversion tracking cookies by setting your browser to block cookies from the `googleadservices.com` domain.

### More information

- [Google Privacy Policy for Google ads and the Google Display Network](https://www.google.com/intl/en/privacy/ads/privacy-policy.html)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/intl/en/privacy/privacy-policy.html)
